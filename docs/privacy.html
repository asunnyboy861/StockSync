Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 4, 2026

1. Introduction

zzoutuo ("we," "our," or "us") built the StockSync iOS application ("App") as a commercial service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use StockSync.

By using StockSync, you agree to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Store API Credentials: Shopify access tokens, WooCommerce consumer keys/secrets. These are stored exclusively on your device in the iOS Keychain and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Support Messages: If you contact us through the in-app support form, we collect your name, email address, and message content.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We do not collect analytics or tracking data. The App does not use any third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Crash Data: We do not collect crash reports. If you opt in to Apple's system-level crash reporting, Apple may share anonymized crash data with us.
  • Device Information: We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the StockSync service (inventory synchronization between your connected stores)
  • To process in-app purchases and manage your subscription
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To communicate important service updates

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 On-Device Storage

All your store credentials, product data, and sync logs are stored locally on your iOS device using:

  • iOS Keychain: For API tokens and credentials (encrypted by iOS)
  • SwiftData: For product, store, and sync log data (stored in your App's sandbox)

4.2 Data Transmission

When StockSync syncs your inventory:

  • Data flows directly between your device and your store's API (Shopify/WooCommerce) over HTTPS/TLS
  • Data does not pass through our servers
  • We have no access to your store data or API credentials

4.3 Security Measures

  • All network communication uses TLS 1.2+ encryption
  • API tokens stored in iOS Keychain with device-only access
  • App Transport Security (ATS) enforced for all connections
  • No credentials stored in source code or logs

5. Third-Party Services

StockSync integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Shopify API: Subject to Shopify's Privacy Policy (shopify.com/legal/privacy)
  • WooCommerce REST API: Subject to your store's own privacy practices
  • Apple StoreKit / App Store: Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/legal/privacy)

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

6. Data Retention

  • Store credentials: Retained on your device until you disconnect the store or delete the App
  • Sync logs: Retained on your device until you clear them or delete the App
  • Support messages: Retained for up to 12 months after resolution
  • Purchase records: Managed by Apple per App Store policies
