Getting Started

1. Connect Your First Store

  • Go to the Stores tab and tap +
  • Choose your platform (Shopify or WooCommerce)
  • Enter your store credentials and tap Connect

2. Review Your Products

  • Navigate to the Products tab to see all synced inventory
  • Use filters to find low stock or out-of-stock items

3. Monitor Sync Activity

  • Check the Dashboard for an overview of stock levels and sync status
  • Tap the bell icon to view detailed sync logs

Shopify Setup

  1. Log in to your Shopify Admin
  2. Go to Settings → Apps and sales channels → Develop apps
  3. Create a new app with the required permissions: read_products, write_inventory, read_inventory
  4. Copy the Admin API access token
  5. Enter your store domain (e.g., mystore.myshopify.com) and the access token in StockSync

WooCommerce Setup

  1. Log in to your WordPress admin
  2. Go to WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API
  3. Click Add key and set permissions to Read/Write
  4. Copy the Consumer Key and Consumer Secret
  5. Enter your store URL and API keys in StockSync

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How fast is the sync?

A: Real-time sync (Pro plan) updates within 30 seconds via webhooks. Auto sync (Starter) runs every 30 minutes. Free plan requires manual sync.

Q: Is my data secure?

A: Yes. All API tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. Your data never passes through third-party servers — it goes directly from your stores to your device.

Q: Can I cancel my subscription?

A: Yes, you can cancel anytime from Settings → Subscription or through the App Store. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

Q: What happens if sync fails?

A: StockSync automatically retries failed syncs. Check the Sync Activity log for details. If issues persist, contact support.
