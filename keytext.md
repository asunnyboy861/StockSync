## Description

### Never Oversell Again.

StockSync is the only iOS-native app that keeps your inventory perfectly synchronized across Shopify, WooCommerce, and more — in real time.

**The Problem**: Selling on multiple platforms means inventory doesn't update fast enough. A customer buys your last item on Shopify, but WooCommerce still shows it as available. Result? Overselling, refunds, and angry customers.

**The Solution**: StockSync uses webhook-driven real-time sync to update stock across all your channels within seconds — not minutes.

---

### KEY FEATURES

**Real-Time Inventory Sync**
• Webhook-driven sync updates stock across all channels in under 30 seconds
• Automatic sync when a sale happens on any connected store
• Manual sync trigger for on-demand updates

**Multi-Channel Support**
• Shopify — Full OAuth integration with product and inventory sync
• WooCommerce — API key connection with real-time stock updates
• Etsy & Amazon — Coming soon!

**Oversell Prevention**
• Smart stock reservation prevents selling items already committed elsewhere
• Instant alerts when stock drops below your threshold
• Visual stock level indicators (green/yellow/red) at a glance

**Smart Dashboard**
• Total stock overview across all channels
• Low stock alerts with one-tap access
• Recent sync activity feed
• Quick manual sync button

**Product Management**
• Search and filter products by name or SKU
• Stock level bars showing per-channel availability
• Low stock and out-of-stock quick filters

**Sync Activity Log**
• Complete history of every sync event
• Filter by success, failed, or pending
• Detailed error messages for troubleshooting
